Joins three sources per item: the live ask the bot can see in the in-game Trade Market, Wynnventory's aggregates, and the local price history this dashboard has recorded. The difference between fair value and the ask - after the 5% listing fee - is the delta; the plan moves a fixed amount of capital through the best deltas per day, within your sell slots. Advisory only: it recommends, the bot still needs an explicit confirmation to buy anything.
