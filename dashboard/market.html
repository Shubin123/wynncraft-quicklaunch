Trade Market

Price lookup Trend / prediction Slot optimizer Liquidity Trade market Bot controller

Drives the in-game Trade Market with the connected bot: walks to the market NPC, opens the auction window, and reads its panes. Panes are matched by name and lore rather than by slot number, so a Wynncraft layout change shows up as an unrecognised pane instead of a wrong click. Buying spends real emeralds and is never automatic - every purchase needs an explicit confirmation and an optional price ceiling.
